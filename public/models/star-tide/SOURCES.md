# Coast Rocks 01 · cropped shore

Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/coast_rocks_01
License: CC0 https://polyhaven.com/license
Authors: Rob Tuytel (photogrammetry), Rico Cilliers (cleanup).

Official 2K glTF and texture files were downloaded and checked against the API MD5 values. Source records are in assets/source/star-tide/coast/download-manifest.json. scripts/prepare-coast-sample.py retains the central 19m survey strip, decimates to 24,000 triangles, retains original UVs, reduces maps to 1K JPEG and exports Draco GLB. The editable Blender source is retained. The game scales two local patches and applies a world-height damp margin.
